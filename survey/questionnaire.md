# Managerial User Study: Questionnaire

**Title:** Would you trust an AI to tell you which borrower might default?
**Tool reviewed:** ABC Ltd Loan Default Risk Advisor (Streamlit app)
**Format:** Google Form, about 8 minutes. Respondents first try the app themselves or watch a 3-minute demo, then answer.
**Consent line (top of form):** *This survey is part of an academic assignment at IRMA. Responses are anonymous and used only for this study. You may skip any question.*

**Who to invite:** branch managers, credit or loan officers, relationship managers, credit or risk heads, managers of cooperative banks or credit societies, MFI/NBFC entrepreneurs, and other experienced decision-makers.

## Section C: Scenario

**C1.** A long-standing customer from your area applies for a loan. You know the family and believe they will repay, but the tool gives a **68% (High)** default risk. What would you most likely do?
- Follow the tool: reject or refer the application
- Follow my judgement: approve on normal terms
- Approve with conditions (guarantor, collateral, shorter tenure)
- Verify more first (bank statements, bureau report, field visit)
